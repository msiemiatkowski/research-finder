Find the studies and the data behind them, and keep everything organized

Research Finder searches research papers and open datasets together, in 40 languages, and saves what it finds into your own Google Drive: one folder per search, every result in a spreadsheet, the open PDFs and data files, and a record of how you searched.

Organized in your Google Drive

Every search becomes its own folder: a Results sheet with one row per study or dataset, in the same columns whatever the source, a Files sheet with every file found, and the open PDFs and data files in their own folders, each linked from its row.

Datasets, not just papers

Search DataCite, Zenodo, Harvard Dataverse, OSF and OpenAIRE next to the 270 million studies in OpenAlex, and save the open data files themselves, with a list of every file found and its download link.

Found in every language

Each keyword is expanded with the other wordings studies use for it, read from the studies themselves, and every term is translated into 40 languages, so research written in Polish, Spanish or Japanese is found too.

Repeatable and reportable

A Search details document records your keywords, every term and translation, the filters and the exact queries. Copy the Boolean string for other databases, or export a CSV.
