Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Research Finder is a free, open-source website that searches open indexes of studies and data repositories (source code). It runs entirely in your browser: there is no server of ours, no account, no cookies and no analytics or tracking.

Your searches

The keywords you type, and the terms made from them, are sent from your browser directly to the services that answer them: OpenAlex, Wikidata, Wikimedia's machine translation service MinT (which translates the terms into other languages), DataCite, Zenodo, Harvard Dataverse, OSF (through its search index, SHARE) and OpenAIRE. When you save a search, the DOIs of the studies are also sent to Semantic Scholar to find open versions of the papers, figshare and OSF are asked for the lists of files of their datasets, and the open PDFs and data files are downloaded by your browser from where they are published. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests. Research Finder does not see or keep them.

File relay (optional). OSF, figshare and many publishers do not let web pages download their files. If you connect a file relay under Settings, Research Finder sends the addresses of those files (OSF and figshare files, and the open PDFs your browser could not download itself) to that relay, which fetches each file and passes it straight to your browser, keeping nothing. A relay is run by whoever set it up, for example you, on your own free Cloudflare account (its code). Without one, nothing is sent to a relay and those files are listed with their links.

Settings you choose (languages, an OpenAlex key, an email address, a file relay) and the machine translations of your terms are stored only in your browser (local storage) and can be removed by clearing it. An OpenAlex key or email address is sent only to OpenAlex, with your searches.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you choose “Connect Google Drive”, you sign in in Google's own window and grant one permission, drive.file: it lets Research Finder create files in your Drive and see or change only the files it created. It cannot see any of your other files.

Research Finder uses this permission only when you click “Save to Drive”: it creates a folder called “Research Finder” and saves there, in one folder per search, the results (a Google Sheet), a list of the files found (a Google Sheet), a document with the details of the search, and the open PDFs and data files your browser is allowed to download. The sign-in token stays in your browser's memory for the session (about an hour); it is not stored or sent anywhere except to Google. Research Finder does not collect, store, share or sell any data from your Google account.

You can disconnect under Settings, and remove Research Finder's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files it saved stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Research Finder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
